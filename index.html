Privacy Policy

Money Trace Book
Last updated: August 11, 2026

Money Trace Book is a personal finance app. Your records stay on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and we do not sell or share your information.

Data stored on your device

The app stores expense and income records, categories, currencies, exchange rates, and settings (such as language) in a local database and system settings on your iPhone.

iCloud backup

If you use iCloud backup in the app, a backup file is saved to your iCloud account. We cannot read that file. How Apple handles iCloud data is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Export

If you export records (for example as a CSV file), the file stays on your device until you choose where to send or save it. We do not receive the export.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising or tracking
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send data to us
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or microphone

App Store

The in-app “Write a Review” action opens Apple’s App Store. That is handled by Apple, not by us.

Children

The app does not collect personal data. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

App Store

应用内「去评价」会打开 Apple 的 App Store，由 Apple 处理，不经过我们。

儿童
